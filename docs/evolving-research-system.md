# Future research direction

> Status: planned research direction. No current repository in this atlas implements this system.

The next question grows from the existing projects: how can formal mathematical work remain useful when it becomes longer, more collaborative, and repeatedly handed from one agent to another?

The future system would study the research substrate around proof work:

- what objects are preserved, including goals, candidate lemmas, certificates, unresolved obligations, scripts, traces, and provenance;
- how objects are linked, while separating mathematical evidence from retrieval and communication;
- when a provisional object becomes safe to inherit;
- how a better challenge or handoff is selected after observing a run.

The first experiment should keep the model, Lean toolchain, verifier, budget, and evaluation boundary stable, and change only one substrate choice at a time. The purpose is to learn whether structured state helps continuation and reuse, rather than to assume that a more elaborate organization is better.

This is a research plan to be developed separately. The current atlas only records why the question emerged and leaves room for the next repository, artifact, or negative result to change it.
