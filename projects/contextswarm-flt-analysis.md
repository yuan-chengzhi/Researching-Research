# ContextSwarm / FLT analysis

## Why this card is here

This is the transition in my trajectory from improving an agent's local environment to asking what a group of agents should share and inherit. It is a collaborative research record, so the card separates my role from the complete project.

## Question at the time

Can a large formal-mathematics collaboration turn many local proof attempts, traces, and tool interactions into cumulative progress on a long-horizon problem?

## My role

My work included ProofState tool optimisation, prompt and experiment design, issue fixes, trace and data analysis attempts, figures connected to ProofState and shared-judge discussions, proofreading, and revisions. The exact scope varied by artifact and should be checked against the relevant collaboration record.

I do not claim to have authored the whole swarm, led the overall paper, or created every component. In particular, this card does not attribute the original full implementation or the entire ContextSwarmJudge contribution to me.

## Evidence and limits

The most useful evidence is not a single aggregate result. It is the preserved relationship between proof-state representations, agent actions, verification, traces, and the analysis of what was or was not reused. The project can support claims about apparatus behaviour and about which distinctions became necessary in the analysis. It cannot by itself establish general autonomous mathematical research ability.

## What changed in my research direction

The work made a distinction visible: activity, communication, and a large amount of generated material are not the same as a persistent mathematical state. It pushed the next question toward unfinished work, provenance, handoff, admission, and the meaning of a link between research objects.

## Retrospective relation

At the time, this was not an implementation of the evolving-substrate agenda. I understood the need for better shared state only gradually. In retrospect, the project is the pressure point that connected local tools and feedback to Metron, Persistent Mathematical Worlds, and the current hypothesis about evolving research substrates.

## Next question

Which typed objects and evidence conditions let a successor agent continue a route without treating provisional, stale, or merely retrieved material as accepted mathematics?
