# ICLR 2027 stage output: FLT system and FLTBench

## Place in the trajectory

This is the first paper-stage output of the larger ContextSwarm FLT formalization project. It should be read together with the parent project card, rather than as a separate research direction called “the ICLR project.”

## What the output records

The submission describes the formalization system, the construction of FLTBench, and the evaluation setting used to understand how far the campaign progressed. It is a stage output of an unfinished formalization effort, not a claim that the theorem was fully formalized.

## My contribution

My contribution was on the system, benchmark, experiment, and evaluation side described in the parent card: ProofState/CPS tool optimisation, prompt and run improvements, FLTBench issue fixing, and assessment of mathematical proof progress and next optimization locations. The manuscript writing is not attributed to me in this card.

## What this establishes

It records a concrete benchmarked stage of the FLT campaign and makes the project's system-level questions inspectable.

## What this does not establish

Submission does not imply acceptance. The output does not establish completion of the FLT formalization or general agent capability.

## Next question

What can the execution traces of this kind of campaign tell us about the structure and reusability of mathematical progress?
