# Agent Math Frontier

## Question

How can a portfolio of mathematical-agent results remain comparable without flattening every outcome into one score?

## What exists

The public repository maintains an evidence-ledger view of open mathematical problems. It distinguishes proof-assistant results, executable specifications, precise informal statements, and research programs. Candidate results are independently reviewed, and the methodology keeps reproducibility visible.

## My role

This project is included as a public evidence and comparison surface. My role in the wider trajectory is to connect its distinctions to the design of the central atlas and to keep the claims bounded.

## Evidence

See the [public repository](https://github.com/yuan-chengzhi/agent-math-frontier) and the [audited commit](https://github.com/yuan-chengzhi/agent-math-frontier/commit/e5fb06928f26e0762e62c17b2cea241c29c40918).

## What this establishes

Different kinds of mathematical outcomes can be recorded without pretending that a formal proof, an executable specification, and a research program have the same evidential status.

## What this does not establish

A portfolio ledger is not a universal causal explanation of agent performance, and one aggregate score cannot faithfully represent every research outcome.

## What changed in my research direction

It supplied a vocabulary for keeping evidence types separate while still making a research portfolio inspectable.

## Next question

How should independent review and portfolio-level comparison interact with persistent research worlds?
